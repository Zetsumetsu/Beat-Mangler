# Teaser

The Beat Mangler is a generative re-composer for the Daisy Patch. Drop in any break, beat, or bar of vocals, and it slices the audio apart and re-sequences it live, with one knob sweeping from faithful playback to total beautiful chaos. Garbage in, music out.

Hiding inside it is Meat Bangler, its feral doppelganger. Hold the encoder on boot and the same engine goes past the edge into pure chaos: experimental noise you can shape and soften into delicate pitched drones.
